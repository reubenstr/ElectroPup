# Assembly
###############################################################################

Notes taken during the assembly process and recommendations.

###############################################################################
# Body
###############################################################################

The carbon fiber rods have PLA inserts on both ends which in turn holds a M5 brass insert.
The PLA inserts attach to the ends of the rod using superglue. 
About five minutes after the superglue is applied the threaded inserts can be applied to the PLA insert.
Superglue will ooze out from the PLA insert which will allow the PLA insert to better form to the rod.

When attaching the carbon fiber rods to the hip plate, add the PCB clips to the lower rods first, then attach the rods loosely.
Place the body on a flat surface and while holding the hip plates down cycle through each rod tightening the rod a little bit each cycle.

###############################################################################
# Motor and Leg Hardware
###############################################################################

Hip:
    motor front: 6x M2.5x12mm
    motor wheel: 2x M3x14mm
    motor wheel: 1x M3x25mm

Upper Leg:    
    motor back: 6x M3x18mm
    motor wheel: 1x M3x12mm
    motor wheel: 2x M3x20mm  
    motor wheel pin: 3x M3 x 8mm
    wireclip upper side: M3x8mm (M3x10mm OK)
    wireclip lower side: M3x8mm (M3x10mm OK)

Lower Leg:    
    motor front: 6  
    motor wheel: 3x M3x20mm
    motor wheel pin: 3x M3 x 8mm

###############################################################################
# Motor Cables 
###############################################################################

Motor cables use 6 position JST-ZH headers on each end and have the following lengths (housing to housing):
4x each: 60mm, 235mm, 465mm

Motor cables are contained in 7mm OD splitloom tubing and have the following lengths:
4x each: 155mm, 465mm

The short length cables are made from the cable that was provided with the motor.
The longer length cable is made from 26AWG silicon wire.

The crimps on the second housing for the two longer cables should be placed into the housing until after the cable is routed through the wire clip and leg.
