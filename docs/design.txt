# MIT Mini Cheetah
###############################################################################

The MIT Mini Cheetah was source of inspiration and it's dimensions are recorded as a sanity check to help ElectroPup's frame stays within common proportions.

Units: mm
Frame width (side to side): 194
Frame length (front hip plate to back hip plate): 304
Frame thickness (top to bottom): 94

Hip width (actuator center ot actuator center): 236.5
Hip length (actuator center to actuator center): 392

Hip center motor to upper leg (actuator center to actuator center): est. 82

Foot width (ball to ball): 256
Foot length (ball to ball): 392

Upper leg length: 212.4
Lower leg length: 205
